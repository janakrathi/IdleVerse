IdleVerse - Privacy notice

IdleVerse has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

To show lyrics, colours and the right design for the song that's playing, it looks up the
CURRENT SONG'S DETAILS online:

  * Lyrics: song title, artist, album and length are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net).
  * Genre and cover art: song title and artist are sent to Apple's iTunes Search API
    (itunes.apple.com).

It also checks GitHub (github.com) about every 6 hours for a newer version of
IdleVerse, and installs it if there is one. Only the app's version number is sent.

Like any web request, these services also see your IP address. Nothing else is sent.

You can turn any of these off at any time: right-click the IdleVerse icon in the
system tray > Privacy.

On your computer, IdleVerse keeps only its settings and a temporary cache of album
covers. It reads what's playing from Windows' media controls.

Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/janakrathi/IdleVerse/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

You can uninstall IdleVerse at any time from Settings > Apps.
